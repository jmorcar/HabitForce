How it works

Simple by design

Four steps to a stronger daily routine.

1
Add your habits

Create any habit — walking, sleep, reading, meditation. Set your daily goal and how you want to verify it.

2
Check in every day

Mark habits complete manually, or let HealthKit verify steps and sleep automatically in the background.

Privacy

Your data stays
on your iPhone

No accounts. No servers. No tracking. Everything lives exclusively on your device.

Zero-collection philosophy

HabitForce has no backend, no user database, and no analytics. All habits, streaks, health data, and reminders are stored only using Apple's on-device frameworks (SwiftData, HealthKit, EventKit). Deleting the app removes everything permanently.

No data collected No internet required No third-party SDKs No account needed
